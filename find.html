Browse all 70 emergency shelters across Barbados. Filter by parish, category and accessibility.

Shelters open only when activated. This page lists every shelter in the booklet — not just the ones currently open. During an active hurricane warning, listen to the local radio or follow the Department of Emergency Management on social media for the official list of open shelters, or call DEM on 438-7575.
