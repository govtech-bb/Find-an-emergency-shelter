What to bring (Emergency Go Bag)

Your Emergency Go Bag should be easy to take with you if you need to evacuate. Stock and refresh it at the start of the hurricane season (1 June).

  • Bottled water
  • Small first aid kit and prescription medication
  • Small flashlight and spare batteries
  • Infant essentials — medicine, sterile water, diapers, ready formula, bottles
  • Important documents (ID, passport, driver's licence) in water-tight plastic bags
  • Cash, preferably in denominations of $20 and less
  • Hand sanitiser and pre-moistened towelettes
  • Personal toiletries and sanitary items
  • A whistle
  • Portable radio and batteries
  • Ready-to-eat food (canned, packaged or boxed) and a can opener
  • Personal identification on your person at all times

A separate Household Disaster Supply Kit (drinking water, two weeks of non-perishable food, tarpaulin, clean-up supplies, fire extinguisher, etc.) should be kept at home for use after a hazard impact.

Before you leave home
  • Listen for evacuation advice on local radio, television and official social media, and leave promptly when advised.
  • Fill as many containers as possible with water — bathtub, laundry and kitchen sinks, and the washing machine.
  • Shut off water and electricity at their main switches.
  • Shut off the valve on large propane gas tanks and anchor them securely.
  • Lock all windows and doors.
  • Make sure your Disaster Supply Kit is stored safely and your Emergency Go Bag is ready.
  • Leave early, in daylight if possible. Avoid flooded areas — never drive through floodwaters.

Pets are not allowed in emergency shelters. Make prior arrangements with friends or family if you have pets, and pack a Pet Survival Kit.

Shelter rules and regulations
  1. The Senior Warden is the supreme authority in the shelter, and his or her decision is final.
  2. An Emergency Senior Warden or other Shelter Warden may take charge in the Senior Warden's absence.
  3. The Warden may call on any occupant to assist; every occupant is expected to cooperate.
  4. If a State of Emergency is declared under the Emergency Management Act CAP 160A, occupants are subject to orders made under the Act.
  5. Wardens and assistants are not responsible for any personal property.
  6. The Department of Emergency Management is not liable for damage to, or loss of, any property.
  7. Any person who defaces or damages the shelter or its furniture will be prosecuted.
  8. Unseemly, indecent or anti-social behaviour, including violence, profane language or drunkenness, will not be tolerated.
  9. Smoking is not allowed.
  10. Consumption of alcohol in any form is strictly forbidden.
  11. Firearms are not allowed.
  12. Offensive weapons are not allowed.
  13. Pets are not allowed.
Protocol for entering shelters
  • Wearing a face mask or fabric face covering is optional.
  • Persons with new onset respiratory symptoms must wear a face mask.
  • Hand sanitisation on entry is optional.
  • If you have been diagnosed with a communicable illness and were isolating at home, declare this on entry and wear a face mask.

Shelters are accessible throughout the Atlantic Hurricane Season (1 June – 30 November) for any emergency that requires them to be opened.
